# Lab Press Grid · 工程套印网格 — Changes from Base

Based on **Swiss Motion Graphics** (`swiss-motion`), borrowing paper mottling, halftone dot fields, redaction bars and spec stamps from **Halftone Dossier** (`halftone-dossier`).

| Section | Base (`swiss-motion`) | Now (`lab-press-grid`) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **§2 Materials & rendering** | Flat, spotless digital surfaces (`#EAE9E5` page, `#FFFFFF` paper); no texture, no shadows, no grain (`mux` grain 0). | **Warm engineering archive paper** (procedural low-frequency mottling, subtle warm vignette, faint centre spine fold and fine paper/ink dust specs) + **45° rotated halftone dot fields** (`multiply` overprint) filling secondary zones, giant numeral backgrounds and data modules. | Breaks the clinical, cookie-cutter digital look of standard flat motion graphics and gives every frame the tactile authority of a 1970s Bell Labs / industrial engineering manual. |
| **§3 Colour logic** | Cool grey page (`#EAE9E5`), pure white paper (`#FFFFFF`), black ink (`#111111`), light grey secondary bars (`#CFCEC9`), and one signal colour (`#E30613`). | **Warm archive paper ground** (`#EDE8DF` outer desk/page, `#F6F3EC` inner sheet), **deep iron-navy primary ink** (`#0F1B29`), **cyan-blue overprint ink** (`#2A6F97`, multiplied for halftone dots and misregistration shadows), and **international safety orange** (`#FF4F00`) as the sole protagonist signal colour. | Replaces the ubiquitous white-and-red template with a distinctive three-ink engineering press palette while keeping the single-protagonist signal rule intact. |
| **§4 Type & subtitles** | Single Latin neo-grotesque (`Archivo`), flat black rendering, minimal 19 px corner notes. | **Tri-font technical hierarchy**: `Noto Sans SC` (weights 500/700/900) for CJK + `Archivo` for Latin display/headers + `JetBrains Mono` for coordinates, rulers and spec indices. **Headlines & giant numerals carry a `+4px / +4px` cyan-blue (`#2A6F97`) multiplied misregistration shadow**. **Four-corner engineering HUD** (`DOC-ID`, scale ratio, live grid coordinate crosshair, beat/bar counter). Subtitles sit flush-left on grid columns with key terms highlighted on safety-orange (`#FF4F00`) solid blocks. | Adds unmistakable print-registration craft and technical blueprint framing suited for tech, business and hardcore explainer videos. |
| **§8 Native moves** | Grid means something, Snap = beat, Programme, Extreme scale contrast, The exception, Knife cut, Re-flow. | Keeps Snap = beat, Knife cut and Extreme scale contrast; borrows and adapts two moves from `halftone-dossier` into strict Swiss grid mechanics: **(1) Redaction Reveal** (solid `#0F1B29` censor bars over key specs/clauses that slice open horizontally on the beat with `cubic-bezier(.7,0,.2,1)`), and **(2) Hydraulic Spec Stamp** (a double-bordered rectangular engineering stamp `[VERIFIED / 核准]` that snaps down on the beat with `+3px` cyan overprint offset and zero cartoon bounce). | Gives tech and business explainers physical "declassification" and "verdict" moves without breaking the non-bouncy, grid-locked Swiss motion discipline. |
| **§7 Sound palette** | Motorik kit / minimal synth + dry print foley (letterpress clack, ruling pen, guillotine). | **120 BPM precision lab pulse** (tight motorik synth bass + grid-mapped marimba/glockenspiel + crisp analog clicks) paired with **heavy industrial press foley**: letterpress clack, caliper slide, redaction tape rip, guillotine cut, and a **hydraulic stamp thud preceded by a half-beat silence drop**. | Matches the heavier physical presence of engineering archive paper, redaction bars and hydraulic verification stamps. |
