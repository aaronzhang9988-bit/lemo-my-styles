# Changes from Crayon Picture Book (蜡笔儿童绘本)

Base: `crayon-book`

## §1 Core concept & default frame size
- **Before**: 16:9 (`1920×1080`) horizontal children's picture book spread with a centre gutter/spine crease and bedtime storybook framing.
- **After**: **3:4 vertical (`1080×1440`) single-page Kyoto-style kraft sketchbook zine (`牛皮纸蜡笔手账 / Kraft Crayon Zine`)**. Designed specifically for vertical book breakdowns, biography stories, life philosophy, and warm business case notes on Xiaohongshu / Douyin / WeChat Channels. The centre spine crease is removed; the vertical page is divided into an upper/middle main crayon illustration & callout stage (top ~72%) and a lower torn-edge 5mm grid-paper note patch (bottom ~28%) held down by translucent washi tape.

## §2 Materials, strokes & paper
- **Before**: Single warm-white cold-press watercolour paper (`#F4EFE6`) across the entire frame.
- **After**: **Three-layer tactile collage surface ("Warm Kyoto Zine Box")**:
  1. **Base stock**: Warm wheat-brown unbleached kraft paper (`#D5BE9E`, RGB `[0.835, 0.745, 0.620]`) with directional wood-pulp fleck and coarse cold-press tooth.
  2. **Torn-edge 5mm grid-paper patches**: Cream-white squared notebook patches (`#F7F3E8`) with fibrous deckle/torn paper edges (3–5 px pale feathering + subtle drop shadow `rgba(43,30,22,0.14)`) and faint 5mm warm-grey/sage grid lines (`rgba(109,140,115,0.22)`).
  3. **Translucent washi tape**: Matcha green (`#6D8C73`) and terracotta red (`#E05A3F`) diagonal-striped Japanese paper tape strips (`alpha ≈ 0.78`) with serrated torn ends pinning corners of the grid patches to the kraft board.

## §3 Colour logic
- **Before**: Children's primary crayons (cadmium red, yellow, cobalt blue) with deep indigo outlines (`#2D3263`), where white crayon (`#FBFAF4`) is invisible until an ultramarine wash sweeps over it.
- **After**: **Warm Kyoto Zine palette & dual-role white wax**:
  - **Outlines**: Deep espresso-roast brown crayon (`#2B1E16`) — warmer and more editorial than indigo.
  - **Luminous White Wax (`#FFFDF7`)**: Because the kraft paper base (`#D5BE9E`) is mid-toned, **creamy white wax crayon is immediately visible as a glowing highlight and solid backing**, while *also* acting as a hydrophobic wax-resist barrier when the watercolour wash passes over it.
  - **Core crayons**: Persimmon red (`#E05A3F`), mustard ochre (`#E8B84B`), sage matcha green (`#6D8C73`), dusty ceramic blue (`#5B7C99`), warm terracotta (`#C86D51`), and soft apricot (`#F2C9A0`).
  - **Watercolour wash**: Warm amber-tea / roasted oolong wash (transmission `[0.58, 0.42, 0.26]`) or deep indigo-tea dusk wash (`[0.24, 0.30, 0.42]`) that pools in the kraft and grid-paper valleys while beading off the white and coloured wax.

## §4 Type & annotation system
- **Before**: Squashed children's storybook lettering (`Gaegu` / `Patrick Hand`) placed directly in blank sky/ground corners.
- **After**: **Study-zine editorial handwriting**:
  - Rounded handwritten Chinese (`ZCOOL KuaiLe`) paired with `Patrick Hand` for Latin/numbers.
  - Main subtitles and key takeaways sit neatly inside the bottom **torn-edge 5mm grid-paper patch**, aligned to the grid rules.
  - Marginalia callouts, numbered badges (`①`, `②`), and short keyword tags sit on the kraft paper beside the illustration, linked by hand-drawn espresso or persimmon-red crayon arrows.

## §7 Sound
- **Before**: Toy piano, music box, flute, clarinet, glockenspiel, and crayon scribble foley.
- **After**: **Acoustic zine desk soundscape**: Nylon-string acoustic guitar / ukulele fingerpicking + kalimba / celesta + warm upright bass pizzicato. Foley adds **crisp washi-tape rip (`tape_rip`)**, **torn grid-paper patch slap (`paper_slap`)**, stick-slip wax crayon scribble (`crayon_draw`), and soft watercolour brush wash (`wash_sweep`).

## §8 Native moves
- **Added / Replaced**:
  1. **White-Wax Highlight & Tea-Wash Resist on Kraft Paper**: Creamy white crayon (`#FFFDF7`) draws visible rim highlights, clouds, steam, or hidden structural secrets directly onto the `#D5BE9E` kraft paper; when the amber-tea or indigo-tea watercolour wash sweeps across, the paper darkens richly while the white wax blazes forth untouched.
  2. **Torn Grid Patch & Washi Tape Slap**: New chapter cards or key takeaway notes slap onto the kraft page as torn 5mm grid patches, immediately pinned down at the corners by strips of diagonal-striped washi tape on the musical beat.
  3. **Live Persimmon-Red Crayon Circle & Arrow Callout**: During an explanation beat, a thick persimmon-red (`#E05A3F`) crayon draws a double hand-looped circle, underline, or curved arrow around the key detail to direct the viewer's eye like a real study notebook.
